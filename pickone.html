Step 1 of 5 — the dump

Who do you think you serve?

All of them. Every audience you've ever told yourself you're for, including the ones you'd be embarrassed to say out loud. One per line. Be greedy here — the whole point of what follows is taking things away, and you can't subtract from a short list.

At least three. If you can only name one, you haven't been honest yet.

Step 2 of 5 — the reality check

Which of these are real?

Not which are attractive. Which ones you have actual evidence for. Answer fast and answer honestly — nobody sees this, and lying to yourself here costs you a year.

Step 3 of 5 — the cut

Keep one. Bury the rest.

Ranked by the evidence you just gave, not by which you like most. Choose the one you're keeping — then write what you're giving up with each of the others. Not "nothing." Something real, or the decision won't hold in three weeks when it gets hard.

Step 4 of 5 — the gauntlet

Now say it so it excludes someone.

A job title isn't an audience. A moment is. Rewrite your survivor as a sentence that describes a specific person at a specific point in their life — and keep rewriting until this page stops arguing with you.

Step 5 of 5 — the consequences

Three questions you can't skip.
