# Starter prospect list

Real people and businesses that publicly advertise positioning or messaging work, found by
web search on 2026-08-29.

## Read this before you use it

**Every entry is unverified.** These came from search results and SEO listicles, and the
sandbox this was researched in could not open any of the sites to confirm they exist, are
current, or still offer what the listing claims. This repo has twice been saved by checking a
secondary source against the primary — apply the same rule here. Open the site, confirm they
still sell this work, and confirm they're independent before you write to anyone.

**Five, one at a time.** Not a blast. The point of stage 1 is watching whether people finish
the tool, and you can't watch fifty. See `OUTREACH.md`.

## Explicitly not your buyer

**April Dunford** and **Andy Raskin** run five-figure executive workshops — Dunford's are
reportedly $50–100K. They have no unmet need your tool addresses, no reason to reply, and
writing to them costs you nothing but teaches you nothing.

The tier you want is the independent running $5–15K engagements who is currently doing the
session on a Miro board. That person has the same problem and none of the leverage.

Same goes for in-house PMMs at large companies: they'll enjoy the tool and they'll never buy
it, because there's no client and no deliverable.

## How to work the list

1. Open the site. Confirm they're independent and still selling this.
2. Find their homepage's audience sentence — the "we help [X]" line.
3. Run it through Pick One. Screenshot or copy the rejection.
4. Send the message from `OUTREACH.md`.
5. Log it: name, source, finished yes/no, where they stopped, exact words.

Five rows. Then stop and read what they told you.
